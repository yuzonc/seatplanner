    • Only Name is required. One row per person, plus-ones included.
    • Subgroup: the circle or household inside the group, like MSoc friends or Ng Kok Long family. Seating keeps each subgroup together first, then the wider group.
    • Tier: main for the main table, vip for front tables, blank for everyone else.
    • Plus one of and Sit with keep people at the same table. Keep apart from puts them at different tables. Separate several names with ;
    • Your current sheet works too: Lincoln +1 (Yong Xuan) becomes Yong Xuan, seated with Lincoln.
